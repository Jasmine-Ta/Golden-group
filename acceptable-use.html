03 / USER RESPONSIBILITIES

Acceptable IT Use Policy

Use facility technology responsibly, securely and productively.

Purpose

This policy provides guidance on the appropriate use of facility computers, networks, software, accounts and information. Users must follow the facility's official rules and authorised instructions.

✅ Acceptable Activities

  • Use systems for authorised work and research.
  • Access approved websites and applications.
  • Use official communication channels appropriately.
  • Store files in approved locations.
  • Report technical problems promptly.

⛔ Prohibited Activities

  • Sharing passwords or login credentials.
  • Installing unapproved software.
  • Accessing information without permission.
  • Bypassing security controls.
  • Copying or disclosing confidential data without authorisation.

👤 Employee Responsibilities

  • Follow IT and security policies.
  • Protect accounts and sensitive information.
  • Use equipment carefully.
  • Respect software licences.
  • Return equipment when required.

🔎 Monitoring and Compliance

Use of facility systems may be monitored in accordance with approved organisational policies and applicable law. Users should not expect personal use of organisational systems to be automatically private.

Reporting Policy Violations

  1. Report suspected misuse to the IT Manager or designated management representative.
  2. Provide relevant information without unnecessarily sharing confidential data.
  3. Allow authorised personnel to investigate the concern.
  4. Follow the facility's approved disciplinary and incident-handling procedures.
