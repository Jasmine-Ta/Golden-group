04 / PROCUREMENT

IT Acquisition Procedures

A structured process for requesting, reviewing and obtaining IT equipment and software.

Purpose

To help ensure that IT purchases meet operational needs, security requirements, technical standards and approved budget and procurement rules.

Acquisition Workflow

Step 1: Identify the Need

The requesting employee describes the equipment or software required and explains the business or research need.

Step 2: Submit a Request

Complete the approved purchase request and provide specifications, quantities and supporting justification.

Step 3: Technical Review

The IT team checks compatibility, security, support requirements and suitability for the facility's systems.

Step 4: Budget and Procurement Review

The relevant personnel confirm funding and apply the organisation's procurement procedures.

Step 5: Obtain Approval

Obtain documented approval from the designated authorised person before purchasing.

Step 6: Purchase and Inspect

Authorised personnel complete the purchase and check that the delivered items match the order.

Step 7: Record and Deploy

Register applicable assets, configure devices securely, install approved software and provide required user guidance.

Required Documentation

  • Purchase request.
  • Business justification.
  • Technical specifications.
  • Quotations where required.
  • Approval records.
  • Purchase order and invoice.
  • Asset register entry where applicable.

Responsibilities

  • Requesting employee: explains the need.
  • Department manager: reviews business requirements.
  • IT team: assesses technical and security suitability.
  • Finance/procurement: applies purchasing rules.
  • Authorised approver: approves according to delegated authority.

Important Control

Do not commit the facility to a purchase before the required approvals have been obtained. Follow the facility's official procurement policy if it differs from this general workflow.
