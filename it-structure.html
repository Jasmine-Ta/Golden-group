IT Department Structure

Roles, responsibilities and escalation within the facility's IT environment.

IT Roles and Responsibilities

The following is a proposed structure. Confirm the job titles and reporting lines against the facility's actual organisational structure.

Facility Management

Provides organisational oversight, approves resources and supports important IT decisions.

IT Manager

Coordinates IT operations, service delivery, security and incident escalation.

Network and Systems Administrator

Maintains servers, network connectivity, system accounts and infrastructure.

Cybersecurity Officer

Supports security monitoring, incident response and compliance activities.

Service Desk Technician

Logs support requests, troubleshoots faults and communicates with users.

Laboratory IT Support

Assists with approved laboratory computers, printers and research equipment.

Reporting and Escalation

  1. Users report technical issues through the approved service desk channel.
  2. The service desk records the issue and performs initial troubleshooting.
  3. Complex issues are referred to the relevant IT specialist.
  4. Major outages and security incidents are escalated to IT management.
  5. Actions and resolutions are documented for future reference.

Key Principles

  • Responsibilities should be clearly assigned.
  • Confidential research information must be protected.
  • Staff should communicate progress to affected users.
  • Only authorised personnel should make high-impact system changes.
