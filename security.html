02 / PROTECTION

Information Security Guidelines

Protect research information, systems, devices and user accounts.

🔑 Password Security

  • Use long, unique passwords or passphrases.
  • Never share login credentials.
  • Enable multifactor authentication where available.
  • Report suspected account compromise immediately.

📧 Email and Phishing

  • Check the sender before opening attachments.
  • Avoid suspicious links and unexpected requests.
  • Never enter credentials on unverified pages.
  • Report suspicious messages to IT support.

💻 Devices and Software

  • Use authorised devices and applications.
  • Install approved security updates.
  • Lock your screen when leaving a workstation.
  • Do not connect unknown USB devices.

🗄️ Data Protection

  • Access only information needed for your role.
  • Store data in approved locations.
  • Do not disclose confidential information without authorisation.
  • Follow approved backup and retention procedures.

Security Incident Reporting

  1. Stop the unsafe activity if it is safe to do so.
  2. Report the incident to the designated IT contact immediately.
  3. Provide the affected device, time, symptoms and relevant details.
  4. Follow instructions from authorised responders.
  5. Do not delete evidence or attempt risky repairs yourself.

Protection of Personal Information

Handle personal information according to applicable legislation, including POPIA where relevant, and the facility's approved policies. Share information only with authorised recipients through approved channels.
