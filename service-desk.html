05 / TECHNICAL SUPPORT

IT Service Desk

Report problems, request assistance and track technical issues.

📝 How to Report an Issue

  1. Use the facility's approved service desk channel.
  2. Describe the problem and when it started.
  3. Include the device, location and error message.
  4. Explain how the issue affects your work.
  5. Record the ticket reference number.
  6. Provide updates when requested.

🎧 Resolution Process

  1. The technician records and categorises the issue.
  2. Initial troubleshooting is performed.
  3. Complex problems are escalated to the relevant specialist.
  4. The technician communicates progress.
  5. The resolution is tested and documented.
  6. The ticket is closed according to procedure.

Ticket Priority Guide

This is a suggested guide. Use the facility's approved priority definitions and response targets where available.

PriorityExampleAction
CriticalMajor outage or suspected security incident.Report immediately and escalate through the urgent reporting process.
HighImportant service affecting several users.Prompt assessment and escalation.
MediumA problem affecting one user or a limited service.Handle according to operational priority.
LowGeneral request or minor inconvenience.Process through the normal service queue.

Information to Include

  • Your name and approved contact details.
  • The affected computer, application or service.
  • The time the problem started.
  • Any error message or troubleshooting already attempted.
  • The impact on work or research activities.

Security Reminder

Do not include passwords in tickets. Report suspected data breaches or cyberattacks immediately through the designated security incident channel.
